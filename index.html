About InAmigos

A Beacon of Hope
in Every Community

Founded on September 23, 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, InAmigos Foundation is a government-licensed Section 8 non-profit organisation headquartered in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh. We believe a charity must focus on children, animals, women, and the environment.

We operate across Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, and Madhya Pradesh — touching lives through a systematic, programmatic approach and mobilising thousands of volunteers nationwide.

Our Initiatives

6 Projects, One Mission

Each project is designed to address a core societal need — from hunger and illiteracy to environmental degradation and gender inequality.

🤝
Project SEVA
Food & Clothing Relief

Distributing food, clothing, and essential supplies to underprivileged communities. Our volunteers ensure no one sleeps hungry or without warmth. Regular food drives and clothing camps are conducted across multiple cities.

Thousands of meals served every month
📖
Bachpanshala
Children's Education

Providing quality education and a joyful learning environment to underprivileged children. We create spaces where happiness meets knowledge — empowering every child with the tools for a brighter future.

Free education for hundreds of children
👩‍💼
Project UDAAN
Women Empowerment

Empowering women through skill development, vocational training, and financial independence programs. We help women take flight toward self-reliance and leadership in their communities.

Women gaining livelihood skills daily
🌱
Project PRAKRITI
Environmental Conservation

Planting saplings and promoting sustainability one tree at a time. Our volunteers heal and protect Mother Earth through tree-planting drives, cleanliness campaigns, and eco-awareness programs.

Thousands of saplings planted
🐾
Project JEEV
Animal Welfare

Protecting stray and wild animals through feeding drives, medical aid, and rescue operations. We believe every living being deserves safety, care, and compassion regardless of their species.

Countless animals rescued & cared for
🚀
Project VIKAS
Youth Skill Development

Training youth in digital and professional skills through India's largest internship program. Over 30,000 interns have been developed under this flagship initiative, building the next generation of changemakers.

30,000+ interns trained across India

Social Purpose

Why We Do This

India faces critical challenges that demand collective action. InAmigos bridges the gap between need and support.

📚
Education Poverty

Millions of children are denied education due to poverty. We break this cycle through free schooling and learning support.

🍚
Hunger & Malnutrition

Food insecurity affects millions daily. Our SEVA drives ensure no one in our communities goes to bed hungry.

👩
Gender Inequality

Women continue to face barriers in opportunity and income. We empower women with skills to achieve financial independence.

🐘
Animal Cruelty

Stray and wild animals suffer daily. Project Jeev rescues, feeds, and provides medical care to animals in need.

🌳
Deforestation

Rampant tree-cutting harms our planet. Prakriti plants saplings and raises eco-awareness to restore green cover.

💼
Youth Unemployment

Skill gaps leave youth behind. Project Vikas bridges this with professional training and real-world experience.
